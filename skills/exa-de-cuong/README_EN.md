[Tiếng Việt](README.md)

## What it does

Drafts, revises, or audits Vietnamese institutional research proposals, master's/specialist/residency and doctoral thesis proposals, full thesis structure, and ethics dossiers.

Standalone skill that replaces the frozen `nature-proposal-writer`.

> Community edition: detailed outline and objective–design–sample-size alignment table. Full drafting, audit, doctoral proposals, and ethics dossiers are in Exacta Pro.

## Example prompt

```text
Use exa-de-cuong to draft a Vietnamese master's thesis proposal for my clinical study.
```

## Shared rules

- Instructions are English; conversation with the user is Vietnamese.
- No manuscript or patient data is sent to external services.
- No invented data, references, or approval numbers; gaps are marked `[CẦN BỔ SUNG: ...]`.
